## Full description

FitTrack is the all-in-one fitness companion for tracking what you eat, how you train, and
how far you've come — without the clutter.

**Nutrition, made fast**
Log meals in seconds. Scan a barcode for instant nutrition info, save your go-to meals for
one-tap logging, and see your calories and macros update in real time against a target built
specifically for your goal.

**Workouts that guide you**
Plan your week with proven training splits — Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs, full-body, and more
— or build your own. During a session, a guided player walks you through each exercise with
video demos, rest timers, and live set logging, so you're never guessing what's next.

**Progress you can actually see**
Track your weight, steps, sleep, and body measurements over time. Watch your strength climb
with estimated one-rep-max charts. Take progress photos and compare before/after side by side.
Streaks and adherence stats keep you honest without being preachy about it.

**Your data, everywhere you are**
Create an account and your entire history — meals, workouts, measurements, photos — follows
you across every device you sign into.

**Free trial, then a simple subscription**
FitTrack includes a 14-day free trial with full access to every feature. After the trial,
continue with a [[weekly/monthly/annual]] subscription of [[price]], auto-renewing unless
cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in your
[[App Store / Google Play]] account settings. Payment is charged to your [[Apple ID / Google
Play]] account at confirmation of purchase.

## Screenshots needed

Both stores require screenshots at specific device sizes. Recommended shot list (5-6 screens
tells the full story without padding):

1. Overview — the daily dashboard with today's stats
2. Nutrition — calorie ring + macros, mid-day with real entries logged
3. Guided workout player — mid-exercise, rest timer or set-logging visible
4. Progress — trend charts (weight or strength) with a few weeks of data
5. Plan Ideas — the recommended-plan card expanded
6. (Optional) Progress photos before/after comparison

Required sizes (check current Apple/Google specs before generating, they do change):
- iPhone 6.7" (1290×2796) — App Store, required
- iPhone 6.5" (1284×2778) — App Store, required if no 6.9"/6.3" set supplied
- iPad 12.9" (2048×2732) — only if `supportsTablet` is enabled (currently off)
- Android phone (1080×1920 or higher) — Play Store, required
- Android 7" / 10" tablet — only if targeting tablets

Real device screenshots (or simulator, at the exact resolution) generally convert better than
marketing composites for a first submission — plain screens of the actual app are enough.
